How FastSpaceSwitch works

By Hadi Al-Musawi. Updated November 2, 2026.

FastSpaceSwitch sends a simulated trackpad swipe with high velocity and minimal movement. The macOS Dock switches desktops without the usual slide.

When you press Ctrl+Left or Ctrl+Right

  1. The app registers Ctrl+Left and Ctrl+Right with macOS. macOS sends those shortcuts directly to the app.
  2. It checks whether there's a desktop to switch to.
  3. It sends the three swipe events the Dock expects: began, changed, and ended.

The installer copies the app to your Applications folder, sets it to open when you log in, and turns off the built-in Ctrl+Arrow Space shortcuts to prevent shortcut conflicts. It saves your previous shortcut settings in Library/Application Support/FastSpaceSwitch. The uninstaller restores them.

Dock animation setting

The installer sets the Dock's expose-animation-duration preference to zero. This can affect how Mission Control and App Exposé open. It saves your previous setting in Library/Application Support/FastSpaceSwitch and restores it when you uninstall. This Dock setting is undocumented, so Apple may change how it works.

Why only macOS 26?

The app uses private macOS features. Apple may change them. macOS 27 changed the swipe format, and this version does not support that format.
