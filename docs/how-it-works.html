How FastSpaceSwitch skips the slide

By Hadi Al-Musawi. Updated November 1, 2026.

FastSpaceSwitch sends a simulated trackpad swipe with high speed and very little movement. The macOS Dock switches desktops without the usual slide.

From shortcut to desktop

  1. The app registers Ctrl+Left and Ctrl+Right with macOS. It receives those shortcuts rather than watching every key.
  2. It checks whether another desktop exists in that direction.
  3. It sends the three swipe events the Dock expects: began, changed, and ended.

The installer turns off the built-in Ctrl+Arrow Space shortcuts so they do not compete with the app. The uninstaller turns them back on.

Ctrl+Up and Ctrl+Down

These stay macOS's own Mission Control and App Exposé shortcuts. The installer sets the Dock's expose-animation-duration preference to zero. It saves the old value and restores it when you uninstall. This Dock setting is undocumented, so Apple may change how it works.

Why only macOS 26?

The app uses private macOS features. Apple may change them. macOS 27 changed the swipe format, and this version does not include the new format.
