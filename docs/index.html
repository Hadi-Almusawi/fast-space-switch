Instant macOS Spaces switching.
Without animation.

Press Ctrl + ← or Ctrl + → to move to the next desktop without the slide.

Download FastSpaceSwitch

Free and open source. Made for macOS Tahoe 26. macOS 27 is not supported.

Get started

  1. Download the ZIP and open it.
  2. Open Terminal. Type cd , drag the extracted folder into Terminal, then press Return.
  3. Run ./install.sh --prebuilt.
  4. Allow FastSpaceSwitch in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility.

Keep the extracted folder. The app runs from there. The download is not notarized by Apple, so macOS may ask you to allow its first launch in Privacy & Security.

What it does

FastSpaceSwitch runs quietly in the background. It uses only Ctrl+Left and Ctrl+Right. It stops at the first and last desktop. Your trackpad swipes stay the same.

It needs Accessibility access to send the desktop switch gesture. The app does not record your other keys, read your screen, or connect to the internet. You do not need to turn off SIP.

Build from source or read how it works.

Need help?

If the shortcuts do not work, check that the app is running and allowed in Accessibility. After a rebuild, remove its old permission entry and add it again.
