What about the app?

FastSpaceSwitch does not connect to the internet. It registers Ctrl+Left and Ctrl+Right and sends a desktop-switch gesture when you press one of them. It does not record other keys or read your screen.

macOS requires Accessibility access for this gesture. You can remove that permission in System Settings. The installer creates the app on your Mac and changes two shortcut settings. Uninstalling restores those shortcuts.
