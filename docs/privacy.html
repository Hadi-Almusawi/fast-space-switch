What about the app?

FastSpaceSwitch does not connect to the internet. It registers Ctrl+Left and Ctrl+Right and sends a desktop-switch gesture when you press one of them. It does not record other keys or read your screen.

macOS requires Accessibility access for this gesture. You can remove that permission in System Settings. Ctrl+Up and Ctrl+Down stay macOS shortcuts and do not need the app's permission.

The installer changes two desktop shortcut settings and the Dock's window-view animation time. It saves your previous Dock value in Library/Application Support/FastSpaceSwitch. Uninstalling restores the settings.
